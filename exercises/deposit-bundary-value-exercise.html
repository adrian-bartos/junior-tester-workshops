Business rules (assumed for this exercise — USDT)
  • Amounts must be numeric and follow currency precision/step (2 decimals for USDT).
  • Amount must be >= min and <= max (boundaries inclusive).
  • USDT: min = 1.00, max = 10000.00, step = 0.01.

BVA test cases (suggested)

For USDT: min-1, min, min+1, nominal, max-1, max, max+1 plus invalid formats.

Quick tips

  • Focus on values just below and above each boundary.
  • Confirm if limits are inclusive: min and max should be accepted.
  • Test precision (too many decimals) and malformed inputs (commas, scientific notation).
